## Decisions already made — do not relitigate
- **Gate hours scale with par instead of being fixed.** `REFERENCE_PAR` $100M,
  `FIXED_EFFORT_FRACTION` 0.45, `EFFORT_SCALE_EXPONENT` 0.5 — 45% of effort is
  fixed regardless of size, the rest grows with the square root of par. Rules
  out the flat hour table, which billed a $20M series the same as a $300M one
  and showed a nonsense effective hourly at both ends. `HAND_SET`.
- **POM hours are derived, not asserted.** `pom_engine.py` builds them from 23
  catalogued sections under a declared drafting model. Rules out the legacy
  `196` figure, which no one could source. The legacy number is retained as
  `LEGACY_HAND_SET_POM_HOURS` so the delta stays visible rather than being
  quietly overwritten.
- **Bond counsel drafts the POM; NEST does not.** `COUNSEL_RESERVED` sections
  are reserved in code, not in prose. Rules out the `nest_drafts_all` model as
  a live option and closes the "can we save the counsel fee" question —
  Volume 1 had already answered it.
- **The document package is the design, and it is locked.** 60 documents, ten
  silos, each joined to a fee gate. `/nest-docs` refers to *this* design, not to
  writing style; writing style moved to `/nest-house-style`. Rules out per-deal
  improvised checklists.
- **Reachability is weighted highest (30%) in the platform self-audit.** An
  unreachable module is absent from the product at any level of quality, so it
  cannot be traded off against test coverage or a pretty screen. Rules out
  scoring a module well because it is well-written.
- **The platform is scored by the platform's own method.** Weakest-link blended
  with average preparedness, the same model `success_predictor` applies to
  deals. If the method is good enough to price a client's chance of closing it
  is good enough to grade us.
- **$275/hr is a floor, not a target.** It is the regulatory proof that fees
  are for work performed. Value sets price. Rules out defending a fee by
  reciting an hour count — and rules out discounting because the platform made
  the work faster. Per Bible Silo 5.
- **Equity in lieu of fees is priced against *development* fees, not success
  fees.** Development fees are not transaction-based, so equity swapped for
  them inherits that character. Break-even 1.0–1.9%. Rules out the simpler
  "equity instead of the success fee" structure, which imports
  transaction-based-compensation risk pre-licence.
- **Bill on par actually issued at each pricing.** The Master Prospectus
  contradicts itself — Part 4 sources $413.25M of bonds (29.4%), Part 8 prices
  each phase as if 100% bond financed, a 3.4× discrepancy inside one document.
  Rules out quoting either figure as authoritative.
- **File cabinet entries are append-only.** A wrong entry is corrected in the
  *next* day's entry with a link back, never edited in place.
